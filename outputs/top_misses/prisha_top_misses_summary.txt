Prisha Week 6 Summary - Top 10 Misses

Task:
Identify the country-year rows where the model is most wrong using residuals.

Residual definition:
Residual = Actual - Predicted

Interpretation:
- Positive residual = the model underpredicted diabetes
- Negative residual = the model overpredicted diabetes
- Large absolute residual = the model was very wrong

Key Findings:
- The model’s largest underpredictions occur mostly in Palau across multiple years, suggesting that diabetes prevalence there is consistently higher 
than the model expects from obesity-based features alone.
- The model’s largest overpredictions occur mostly in the United States and Canada across multiple years, 
suggesting that obesity-based features may overestimate diabetes prevalence in those countries.
- These repeated multi-year patterns suggest persistent country-level or region-level effects rather
 than random one-year noise.
- Overall, the residual analysis shows that obesity is a useful predictor, but not 
sufficient by itself to explain diabetes prevalence across all countries.

Why this matters:
- Big misses may suggest hidden confounders beyond obesity alone
- Misses clustered in one region may suggest geography-specific drivers
- Underpredicted rows mean diabetes is higher than the model expected
- Overpredicted rows mean diabetes is lower than the model expected
